Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

Straight answers — no fluff.

Do you work with practices outside of Los Angeles? +
Absolutely. While we're based in Los Angeles and built our reputation in one of the most competitive aesthetic markets in the world, we work with practices across the United States and internationally — including Germany and Europe. Location is no barrier. Everything we do is remote-first and built for high-performance from anywhere.
What exactly is included in "ad spend" — and who controls it? +
Ad spend is the budget that goes directly to Meta, Google, or TikTok to serve your ads — it's separate from our management fee and always 100% controlled by you. You set the budget, you own the ad accounts, and you see every dollar in real time. We build, manage, and optimize the campaigns. You fund them. No surprises.
How long before I start seeing real results? +
Paid advertising typically generates consultation inquiries within the first 2–4 weeks. Organic growth — followers, reach, brand authority — compounds over 3–6 months and accelerates from there. One of our clients went from $17K to $147K in monthly revenue over 7 months. We track everything weekly so you always know exactly where you stand.
Do you only work with plastic surgeons? +
Plastic surgery is our flagship specialty, but we work across the full medical aesthetics space — cosmetic dentistry, medical spas, dermatology, anti-aging and longevity practices, hair restoration, wellness brands, and more. If your patients are choosing you based on how you look online, we're the right fit.
What does the branding process look like? +
We start with a deep discovery session — your vision, your ideal patient, the aesthetic you want to project. From there we build your logo, color palette, typography, brand voice, and a full style guide. You're involved at every decision point, and we iterate until it's exactly right. Most brand identity projects are complete within 2–3 weeks.
Can I just start with one service and add more later? +
Yes — and many clients do. Some start with brand identity only, then layer in social media once the foundation is right. Others start with paid ads to generate immediate bookings while organic content builds in the background. We'll recommend what makes sense for your practice and your budget, and scale from there.
How is CM Strategic Marketing different from a general digital agency? +
We work exclusively with medical practices — no restaurants, gyms, or lifestyle brands. That means we already understand patient psychology, compliance requirements, and the visual standards that separate elite practices from the rest. We don't have a learning curve on your industry. We also do everything in-house — no outsourced content, no freelancers, no quality gaps. And we measure success in consultations booked and revenue generated, not follower counts.
